Activity: In the Training Module, add a diary reminder in a few day's time for Nick Crabbe (as the user), and add yourself as the worker. You can set it for a carer or a child record, and be as creative as you like with the title and notes.
Your Outlook Diary remains the primary organisational tool, which is considered mandatory to be kept up-to-date. Use of the CHARMS diary is an additional tool, which comes under the best practice guidance. Speak to your Manager and Team Colleagues about how the CHARMS diary is utilised in everyday practice.
